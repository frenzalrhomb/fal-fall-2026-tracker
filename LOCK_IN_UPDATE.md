## The crucial scoring correction

The FAL rule adds +0.5 for **each user whose list says Watching or Completed at each weekly cutoff**, plus an additional +0.25 on even weeks as written in the user-supplied rules. Registration closure does **not** freeze or reset this number. If 6,047 users are still Watching or Completed at every cutoff, a title active for all 13 weeks earns approximately **6,047 × (13×0.5 + 6×0.25) = 48,376 audience points from these users alone**, even if it adds zero new viewers. Completed remains eligible; users changing to Dropped cease to contribute and cause a separate negative term. A title on the bench scores zero until promoted.

Wild Last Boss S2 was the only promising candidate with a full early episode observation at cutoff: 23,400 MAL members, 6,047 Watching + Completed, 7.60 score from 924 ratings, 32 Dropped, +3,841 members and +2,181 AniList in roughly 25 hours after release. Its baseline is **real and recurring**, not just a pre-registration jump that FAL forgets. Its first-episode acceleration cannot fairly be compared with shows awaiting their premieres. The 7.60 is early and may change before the Week 3 score event. On the sample and season-long evidence currently available, I narrowly favor Ranma as the fifth starter, **not by treating Wild Last Boss's 6,047 as zero**.

Ranma 3 starts Japanese broadcast October 3 at 24:55 JST (October 4 00:55 JST = October 3 15:55 UTC), followed by Netflix streaming. It should be out more than a day before October 4 22:00 UTC Week 1 scoring. Its 26,786 MAL members exceed Wild Last Boss's 23,400, the preceding anime had a 77/100 versus Wild Last Boss predecessor's 74/100 AniList score, Netflix availability is clear, and Ranma S2 placed #6 with 368,893 FAL points in the user's Fall 2025 screenshot (Wild Last Boss S1 #9 with 346,152). Those earlier totals are context, **not calibrated forecasts under 2026 rules**. The margin between these two is uncertain, and Wild Last Boss could outperform Ranma if the observed early reception and audience persist. The chosen roster seeks stable 13-week upside with the limited swap budget, rather than maximizing an unequal early snapshot.

## Swap discipline

Do not commit to swapping simply because a bench title debuts. Around Week 2 and the first rating checkpoint (Week 3), compare actual MAL Watching + Completed, score, Dropped and forum engagement across active and bench. Firefly's source strength, the two large sequel audiences and Appraisal/Ranma's conversion determine the first promotion. Only four ordinary swaps can be used, at most one each week. Continued Completed status still earns weekly points, so never bench solely because a series has ended. Use aces based on eligible *weekly team points* and the 60,000 Watching + Completed limit, not pre-season MAL members. The +10,000 booster is the no-friction Week 10+ wildcard unless an extra swap creates more than 15,000 net gain relative to it.
