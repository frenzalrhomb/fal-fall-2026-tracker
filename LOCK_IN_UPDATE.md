Two changes from the earlier roster: **Ranma replaces Seitokai among starters; Love-Potion replaces Dragon Ball on the bench.** Rights announcements do not themselves generate FAL points. They affect our estimate of conversion from Plan to Watch to Watching or Completed across the MAL audience. There is no measured US share of FAL users in this dataset, and these are judgment calls under uncertainty, not a fitted geographical model.

### Why Ranma over Seitokai

Ranma has 26,439 MAL members, 10,824 AniList users, an AniList prior-season reception score of 77/100, and a Netflix announcement explicitly covering worldwide availability. It premieres before Week 1. Seitokai has stronger recent growth (+650 against Ranma +256), 24,083 MAL members, and 14,144 AniList users. Its Japanese publisher confirms Netflix **in Japan** from October 6, but we found no confirmed US release and it is absent from Crunchyroll's published fall lineup. Since an active title has to earn Week 1 points, the US release uncertainty tips this close comparison toward Ranma. Seitokai can still win if an American distributor announces a prompt release; absence of an announcement does not prove no future license.

### Why Love-Potion over Dragon Ball

Dragon Ball has a larger preseason audience (28,868 MAL/11,205 AniList versus Love-Potion's 21,053/8,619), a trailer with substantial English-comment activity, and a higher Anime Corner poll rank. Love-Potion has faster measured growth (+555 MAL versus +320), and Crunchyroll confirms an October 5 launch worldwide outside Japan and China. As of this audit, Dragon Ball Super: Beerus has a confirmed Japan premiere but **no confirmed US stream for this new edition**. The original Dragon Ball Super streaming on Crunchyroll does not settle that question. The US access risk tips the narrow final bench slot to Love-Potion. Both bench candidates miss Week 1, so this change does not alter the initial active five.

### Other rights and risk

Sword S2 is confirmed HIDIVE exclusive in the US, so it has a smaller distribution route than a Netflix or Crunchyroll title. Its very large MAL and AniList audiences still justify a restricted starter slot. Firefly is confirmed Crunchyroll US, despite the service's announced territory list omitting Southeast Asia. Netflix explicitly says Ramparts S2 launches **worldwide**, correcting the earlier Japan-only qualification.

## September 27 clarification: late premieres and unconfirmed licensing

The recommendation above remains a **narrow judgment**, not a measured platform effect. Love-Potion is selected for its collected evidence, not the user's personal interest. Seitokai remains a serious alternative with stronger current MAL/AniList momentum.

Dragon Ball Super: Beerus premieres October 11 at 23:15 JST / 14:15 UTC. This is Week 2, 7h45 before the 22:00 UTC scoring cutoff at broadcast start; viewing and later streaming leave less time. It misses Week 1, but benching it lets another title score, so its late premiere does not automatically cost the team a scoring slot. The risks are weak initial conversion, delayed usefulness of a scarce bench slot, and spending one of four regular swaps if it is promoted. Twelve uninterrupted weekly episodes from October 11 reach December 27; thirteen reach January 3, after FAL. Episode count and interruptions are not confirmed here. A finale within the FAL window may affect catch-up viewing, scores, favorites and discussion. Changing Watching to Completed does not itself add audience points because both count.

Seitokai has legal Japanese broadcasts and streams: October 3 at 24:30 JST on TV, dAnime Store and AnimeFesta, followed by Netflix Japan and other domestic platforms from October 6. US availability is unconfirmed, not confirmed absent for the full season. Crunchyroll news coverage is not a license announcement. No Crunchyroll distribution is not synonymous with no official English release, and no US simulcast does not imply zero MAL activity. We have not measured what share of its prospective MAL audience would use unofficial viewing or how fast English subtitles would be available.

Historical limitation: historical_evidence.json contains four transcribed Fall 2025 ranking rows, not a complete cross-season dataset labelled with platform and rights at premiere. It cannot estimate a numerical streaming penalty. The uploaded 2025 table shows SANDA at #10 with 322,653 points; [Amazon's October 2025 announcement](https://www.aboutamazon.jp/news/entertainment/amazon-prime-video-new-content-october-2025) lists Prime Video exclusive distribution. This supports considering platforms beyond Crunchyroll, but does not establish how an anime with no US simulcast would perform. Girls Band Cry had substantial English episode discussion in June 2024 before its November 6 Crunchyroll arrival; we have not verified its final FAL rank and do not present it as a quantified FAL precedent.

Closest alternatives for Dragon Ball's now-vacant bench place, after Ranma is already active: Love-Potion is the current marginal preference for faster growth and a confirmed US October 5 route; Seitokai is the stronger speculative alternative given 24,083 MAL / 14,144 AniList users and +650 latest MAL growth; Yasei S2 merits attention with 19,559 MAL / 10,688 AniList and +503 growth, but its latest airing snapshot was only 16 minutes after the advance premiere; Detective S2 has a much larger MAL base but weaker prior-season AniList reception (61/100). These are not calibrated season-point projections.
