## Love-Potion: promotions versus measured buzz

- The [official anime site](https://horemajo-anime.com/) posted several announcements on September 25, including a [cast programme](https://horemajo-anime.com/news/index00280000.html) at 20:00 JST and an [advance episode-one screening](https://horemajo-anime.com/news/index00270000.html) at 21:00 JST on **October 3**, both on KADOKAWA's YouTube channel. This is a real promotional push, but these future events cannot reveal pre-lock episode reception.
- The September 24 tracker recorded its main Japanese trailer at about 576,000 views, +21,400 in a day; latest bounded comment-language samples skew Japanese. Trailer views are exposure, not MAL Watching.
- X is **not a quantitative tracked source** in this repository. Public indexed posts can flag announcements, but cannot establish a day-over-day posting or engagement surge here. Do not interpret absent X data as no buzz.
- Its 20,000 MAL members versus Returner's 45,926 is a roughly 25,900-person starting gap. At the latest daily gain difference (about 263), closing that gap would take roughly 99 days **if both rates stayed constant**; they almost certainly will not. A higher eventual MAL score or lower drops could change total FAL value. That is why this is a close watch, not an automatic replacement.
